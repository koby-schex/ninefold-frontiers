Great Strider — Motion Study 03

Inspect the actual study mesh in synchronized three-quarter, front and side views. These are provisional movement studies, not final game animations.

Idle and continuous walking loop. Start/stop and the 18° stepping turn play once and hold their final pose. Use Restart to replay; that reset is not an animated transition. Scrubbing pauses playback.

The camera tracks forward travel but keeps a fixed viewing direction during the turn. Ground lines are world-fixed review references. This prototype does not certify physical balance, collision clearance or final skin deformation.

Works offline. No Unity, Blender, account or network connection required.
