# World Five encounter variety — Missions 11–14
New opt-in **World Five encounter variety study** in Unity, with a separate V2 save path. Original V4 tutorial, Act One V1 studies, and their saved battles are not overwritten.

**Mission 11:** two Ashbound — first contact, Rift Forager + Parted Striker.
**Mission 12:** three Ashbound — two Rift Foragers + one Seam Castigator.
**Mission 13:** four Ashbound — Parted Striker + Anchor Wrecker + Camp Witness + Rift Forager.
**Mission 14:** two Ashbound — Afterecho Seer + Seam Castigator; lower enemy quantity but greater ranged status pressure.

This is a **first encounter-composition pass**, not random enemy generation. Counts, unit mix and positions are intentionally authored and regression-tested. A good long campaign should interleave small precision fights, patrols, larger clashes, objective pressure, environmental dangers, story battles and rare extraordinary encounters. Avoid monotonically increasing enemy numbers; scale difficulty with positioning, synergy, terrain and objectives instead. The six Ashbound roles remain ordinary anonymous NPCs, not Apex or collectible units. Ashbound do not appear in tutorial missions 001–010.

**Limitations:** missions 12–14 are defeat encounters only in this study. No new story dialogue or final scenery/model/animation work in this PR. Balancing and full end-to-end victory/defeat path still require Unity playtesting. Separate profile means V1 progress is preserved but not automatically copied.
