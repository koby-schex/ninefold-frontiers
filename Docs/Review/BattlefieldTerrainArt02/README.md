# Battlefield Terrain Art 02 — consolidated battlefield + optional generation

## Delivered
- Five distinct World Five-inspired geographical art studies for all fourteen existing authored encounters: pale survey, violet shelves, mineral basin, broken terrace, deep fracture. They provide different silhouette profiles, spacing, shelf layers, mineral colors and lighting. Neutral Quick Battle has its own palette. No proposed geographical name or biology becomes canon.
- Replacement of the rectangular floor/plinth cubes and raised boundary boxes with continuous triangulated level paving, weathered paired albedo/normal maps, fine mineral marks, layered irregular foundation, differentiated cover strata, and external distant rock shelves. Actual slow-ground areas use subdued gritty chevrons rather than objective-yellow stripes. Gold objective marker logic, targeting, collision and map bounds are unchanged.
- Core art plan generates the actual triangles sent to Unity; one batched mesh/material per slot, maximum 16 environment batches, two reusable 256px mipmapped textures, no individual decoration GameObjects, added lights, colliders or per-frame geometry. Source-owned previous unit models are unchanged.
- Slight deterministic fresh-attempt procedural generation is **enabled for non-narrative Quick Battle** (`fixture-mixed`, expanded layout only). One secondary cover rectangle may shift up to 0.75m per axis, retaining its exact footprint, blocking flags and cover reduction. Central authored cover, spawns, units, bounds, ground cost and objectives remain fixed. Resolved maps are saved as before. Old saves are not regenerated. Seed from actual persisted attempt identifier, platform-independent. Bounded candidate search falls back to template where clearance cannot be proven.
- A later mission beginning at 015 can opt in explicitly when it is a Standard defeat-only encounter with no friendly NPC spawns. No existing 1–14 story or tutorial layout is procedural. There are no later missions yet in the active catalog. No new campaign content, new modes or world collision introduced.
- Quick Battle briefing marks fixed cover only, with a notice that secondary cover varies; actual battlefield and radar read the resolved saved map.
