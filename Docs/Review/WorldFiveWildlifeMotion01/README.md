# World Five wildlife motion — procedural pass 01

Native wildlife in the existing Unity battlefield now animate their assembled body parts in response to authoritative movement and attack events. Seamstalkers use a faster and larger diagonal gait, pronounced forward head strike and lateral tail motion. Sillrunners use shorter strides, a restrained forward head reaction and balance-fan movement. Dorsal body sway reinforces motion without changing root positions.

Animation poses are restored on every authoritative reconciliation, including reduced-motion playback. Creature motion never changes the simulated collision body, hit chance, targeting, health, saves, initiative or mission progression. This is a basic procedural blockout, not production rigging, foot IK, organic deformation or a final art approval. Existing playable-unit models and their parallel workstream remain untouched.

Unity visual review: observe Mission 7 Seamstalkers moving and attacking, Mission 8 Sillrunner zone defense, consecutive animations, clean idle pose after the attack, defeat reaction, and reduced-motion setting.
