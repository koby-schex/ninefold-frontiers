# Ninefold: Frontiers — PR #197 — Expanded Modes

## Scope and canon discipline
**Included:** Campaigns retain all 100 authored World Five missions; Horde, Pathweave (working title, not locked canon) and World Collision expand with playable content. **No PvP.** World One and World Two campaigns are reserved for future canon-approved development, not faked with World Five visuals. Keep Apex Unit, Arcana and The Fractured Meridian terminology.

All prototype environments and enemy templates remain clearly **noncanonical candidates**. Pathweave route consequences are gameplay proposals, not newly asserted cosmological laws.

## Horde — The Unbroken Line
- One uninterrupted saved battle: six defeat-objective waves with 2, 3, 3, 4, 4 and 5 active hostiles respectively (21 total). Five player-triggered preparation intervals provide space for positioning and support abilities before initiating the next wave at a rally marker.
- Future enemies are registered but dormant: noneligible, positive HP, unable to attack, block, or appear as live render targets. When prior wave and preparation objectives clear, reinforcements wake at deterministic, legal spawn positions and enter the next round. Squad HP, location, statuses and used signatures carry forward; **no reset between waves**.
- Uses existing health and eligibility bits, avoiding a breaking binary save revision. One victory and first-clear receipt only after all six waves; defeated/retreated runs yield no first-clear resources. The six old discrete wave pilots remain untouched.
- Active Horde HUD includes wave/preparation guidance and the results view reports wave progress and a deterministic run score. Endless mode, extra interwave power menus and world-wide leaderboards are future work; the six-wave run is playable now, not falsely labeled endless.

## Pathweave — deeper Ninefold consequences
- Existing shelter/containment choice remains exclusive and persists in victory receipts.
- After either ending, a **second exclusive choice** changes the terminal mission and tactical map:
  - Shelter: preserve a witness's testimony OR prioritize the open passage.
  - Containment: verify the record OR defend the unstable boundary.
- Distinct objectives, enemy formations, geometry and consequences; the Missions hub displays the selected route and second outcome. This is not a generic challenge checklist. Expanding the graph beyond World Five requires Master Lore & World-Building Archive continuity review and approved inter-world details.

## World Collision — multi-front exercises
- Adds three seven-deployment six-minimum mixed-faction battles after the two existing pilots. Securing separate flanks, relay stabilization and large-formation defenses require real combat objectives and saved progression gates.
- Existing one-Apex cap stays enforced. Existing native AI, mobile battle engine, local saves and reward policies are reused. These remain abstract exercises until cross-world lore and art assets are approved.

## Compatibility, economy and QA
- Exact existing World Five save revision and original campaign content preserved; old mode gates are re-applied when composing the expanded catalog.
- In total 8 new playable entries: 1 continuous Horde, 4 Pathweave routes, 3 World Collision battles.
- The sequential objective contract supports 11 mandatory ordered stages while preserving all older one-to-three stage snapshots and save bytes.
- Regression tests cover locked future enemies, saved health and enemy activation, actual AI fighting, route forks, seven-unit mixed squads and original content compatibility.
- Full battle difficulty scaling, production unit statistics, model anatomy, device frame rates and final canon certification remain release gates; do not treat this PR as finished game art.
