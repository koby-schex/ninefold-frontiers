# First polished mission: art brief

Status: source-checked production brief, 6 October 2026. No finished models,
approved turnarounds, rendered mission or device performance results are delivered
by this document. Production choices labeled **proposed** remain reviewable.

## Purpose and scope

Bring the opening mission, internal ID `FP-C01-M01`, to production visual quality
in portrait on mobile. The visual target is the archive's **Cinematic Stylized
Realism**: materially grounded, detailed and believable, with strong silhouettes
at the tactical camera distance. Existing primitive playtest actors and terrain
are technical fixtures, not designs to polish into canon.

The approved opening uses Great Strider, Living Veil and Burdenbeast, with no
combat opponents or reinforcements. Its art package needs movement, contextual
survey interaction, environmental hazard communication and a safe destination.
It does not need an invented enemy to make the scene feel populated. Restricted
story causes, destination identity, dialogue and resolution remain in the Project.
The mission's exact layout and coordinates have not been approved.

This is the first visual production slice within [FP-01](FP01.md). FP-01 still
covers three encounters, one enemy definition and one rescue actor overall.
The first mission does not replace that milestone or the campaign scope.

## Environment and camera direction — proposed execution

Build one compact, bounded route layout using a reusable World Five kit. Reusing
materials and modular pieces across later missions is acceptable; repeating an
identical battlefield is not. Each later encounter needs an authored layout.

The playable ground should have a clear entry, readable traversal choices,
interaction positions and destination. Keep the route visually continuous enough
to follow from the portrait camera. Environmental boundaries must agree with
movement constraints. Distant scenery can convey a much larger world without
becoming clickable or hiding navigable edges.

Use the PF6 Continuance material language: weathered mineral-ceramic structures,
flexible shelter systems, ash-silver surfaces and mobile techno-arcane components.
World Five's fractured-dusk lighting and controlled stabilization light should
support its identity. Specific props, sky composition and placement remain design
proposals, not new landmarks, mechanisms or named sites.

Review the composition with Ilyth at full height, Olyra deployed and the carrier
turning. Test foreground occlusion, overlapping partners and HUD clearance before
adding detail. A large unit must not obscure the next legal destination or its
own health display. Do not copy the showcase's settlement wholesale as a mission.

## Interaction and effects

- Keep every unit's health readable without selection, with unambiguous attachment
  to its logical roster entry. Paired figures must not imply two selectable units.
- Preserve clear current-turn, selected-unit, movement-preview and attack-range
  presentation. Attack-range review also belongs in later combat encounters;
  this noncombat mission cannot prove it works with enemies present.
- Show contextual survey readiness and completion separately from abilities.
  Survey is not an extra Signature or a combat shot.
- Separate hazard forecast from the actual debris event using shape, timing and
  restrained effects as well as color. The approved mission includes a response
  window; presentation cannot imply that real-time hesitation causes damage.
- Keep terrain, movement preview and destination visible under effects. Arcana
  effects must communicate an established mechanism. No generic magic overlay
  should be added to compensate for missing animation or unclear interaction.
- UI indicators are game abstractions. They do not establish floating in-world
  technology. Portrait controls must remain legible with touch safe areas.

## Build order and review gates

1. **Reference and scale sheets:** proposed first subject is Great Strider because
   traversal scale and Waycaster interaction are dependencies for this mission.
   Produce reviewed sheets for all three pairings before final geometry. Resolve
   unseen anatomy/equipment questions against S1–S3; request a canon revision only
   if a change to locked material is actually intended.
2. **Scale blockout:** use explicitly temporary proxies at intended meter scale to
   prove layout clearance, camera framing, bounded movement and UI visibility.
   This is a validation tool, not a finished visual deliverable.
3. **One finished pairing:** build Great Strider mesh, materials, rig and required
   motion; review silhouette, partner relationship and survey at tactical distance.
   Its acceptance sets a quality reference, not a shared creature skeleton.
4. **Full mission art:** finish the other pairings, terrain, props, lighting,
   effects and interface assets listed in the [asset checklist](FirstMissionAssetChecklist.md).
5. **Unity and device evidence:** verify in Unity 6.3 LTS (6000.3.21f1), then iPhone
   14 and additional test environments. Record frame time, memory, load time,
   thermal behavior, transparency/overdraw and touch readability. Set quantitative
   budgets from measurements; no polygon, texture or performance promise is locked
   by this brief.

Offline play requires the mission's essential assets locally available. Resume
must display committed state consistently without repeating visual events as new
gameplay effects. Failed attempts must not remove owned units or account progress.

Final acceptance requires side-by-side reference review, neutral/operational
turntables, portrait gameplay captures, clearance evidence and measured device
results. Grayscale readability and reduced visual detail must preserve defining
anatomy and silhouettes. Merging this brief does not certify those gates, change
locked canon or make provisional production choices new lore.
