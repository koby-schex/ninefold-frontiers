# FP-01: first playable milestone

## Status

This PR supplies project scaffolding only. Nothing here is a playable mission,
completed art asset, verified Unity build or release candidate.

## Scope

Three short introductory encounters, three complete starter roster entries,
one enemy definition and one noncombat rescue actor. One environment kit supplies
three authored layouts. The player flow connects mission selection, battle,
results, replay and persistent local progression. Story, units and visuals follow
the authoritative Project archive; restricted production specifics are not copied
into this public repository.

## Ordered milestones

1. **Foundation import:** pinned Editor, URP, repository/LFS rules, generated settings
   and package lock verified in Unity and committed. This PR starts this stage.
2. **Rules and data:** settle remaining kit/geometry/reward inputs; implement the
   command/state core, movement, initiative, deterministic combat and ability timing.
3. **Mission loop:** objective contracts, enemy behavior, portrait controls, three
   encounter layouts, local rewards and interruption-safe snapshots.
4. **Production presentation:** reviewed scale sheets, models, rigs, materials,
   animation, scene lighting, VFX/audio and touch readability.
5. **Device verification:** install through the configured build route, measure and
   correct failures before expanding production.

## Acceptance evidence required later

- All three encounters can finish, fail, retry and resume with no objective softlock.
- Installed content can cold-launch, play and grant rewards with networking disabled.
- Movement/action budgets, turn order, cooldowns and Signature limits follow spec.
- Interruption after rescue, objective progress or rewards cannot duplicate effects.
- Actual large-unit footprints fit required paths without rescaling canonical bodies.
- Active-play duration is measured separately from voluntary thinking and reading.
- No temporary models pass the final visual-fidelity gate.
- iPhone 14 plus additional environments: touch safe areas, loading, memory,
  frame-time and thermal behavior measured. Proposed 30 fps baseline is not verified.

The nine-roster launch and at least 100 missions per faction campaign remain broader
scope; FP-01 is a production milestone. PvP remains a later post-release system.
