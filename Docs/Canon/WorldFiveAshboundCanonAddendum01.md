# World Five — Ashbound Canon Addendum 01
**Canon approved: 2026-10-22.** This is an additive canon record to be incorporated into the next Master Lore archive edition. The published v26.0 file must not be silently overwritten.

## Canon commitments
- **The Ashbound**: hostile splinter Avarin cult inhabiting dispersed survivalist camps around World Five's unstable routes. Principal campaign enemy force, **NOT a playable faction**.
- Initiation rite **the Parting**: aspirant personally kills their own genuinely bonded creature in an act of deliberate betrayal. The rite is disturbing, not glorious or celebratory.
- Arcana/technical result **Fracture Wake**: unstable short-lived enhanced fracture sensing, burst movement and disruption. Source is engineered unstable Arcana capture, *not* stolen souls, forced bonds or a biological bonding function of the Avarin mantle.
- Persistent drawback **the Afterecho**: distorted sensory readings, disorientation, phantom route impressions and harmful instability under stressful conditions. Power has consequences; users are not invincible.
- The Ashbound's six ordinary reusable campaign enemy varieties: **Rift Forager, Parted Striker, Seam Castigator, Anchor Wrecker, Afterecho Seer, Camp Witness**. These are anonymous NPC archetypes with combat variety, **not** named characters, special units, Apex Units or collectible roster entries. Bosses and unique leaders only in separately approved future campaign content.
- Visually remain anatomically Avarin (bipedal body, living crescent sensory mantle, temple-face, environmental perception), differentiated through dark asymmetrical salvage, ritual equipment, missing companion gear and unstable hardware signatures. No bonded creatures fight alongside Ashbound members.
- Wildlife remains distinct from the Ashbound: animal encounters may be neutral, territorial, fearful or hostile in context, never inherently evil.

## Continuity safeguards
The Continuance's creature bonds remain trust-first and consensual. The Avarin mantle senses environment and Arcana, **not** bonds. World Five retains its established geographic region names. No early exposure of the first game's route-crisis mystery, Serai Venn's disappearance or Nethra's origin.

## Implementation boundary
This file locks narrative direction only. Existing abstract `fixture-npc` opponents are not yet converted to Ashbound combatants; their models, abilities, stats and mission integration require separate, tested work. Game implementation may use placeholder technical identifiers without elevating them into lore.
